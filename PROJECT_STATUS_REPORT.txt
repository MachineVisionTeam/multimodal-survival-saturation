EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------

I have systematically tested 10 distinct fusion-architecture interventions and
3 different output-side (loss / attention) interventions on top of two
locked-replicated multimodal cancer-survival baselines (Pathomic Fusion on
TCGA-GBMLGG and TCGA-KIRC; MCAT on TCGA-GBMLGG and TCGA-BLCA). Of the 13
interventions trained, NONE has produced a statistically significant
improvement in concordance index (c-Index) over its respective baseline. The
most recently completed experiment (a Cox-compatible reformulation of
Gaussian Random Fuzzy Number evidential fusion, "GRFN-Pathomic") additionally
falsified a pre-registered calibration-improvement hypothesis with
high confidence (paired-t p = 0.005, Wilcoxon p = 0.0006). Together these
results indicate that the cross-modal interaction signal on TCGA glioma
survival is bounded above by approximately 4% of variance — a property of
the data and the choice of survival likelihood, not of any individual
fusion architecture. The next planned experiment (DSM-Pathomic, mixture-of-
Weibulls survival likelihood) is the loss-function arm of a three-axis
saturation map (architecture / encoder / loss).


PROJECT FRAMING
---------------

Multimodal cancer survival prediction combines whole-slide pathology images
(WSIs), cell-graph features, and bulk genomic profiles into a single per-
patient risk model. Published 2022-2026 work shows c-Index in the 0.80-0.86
range on TCGA-GBMLGG (770 patients, glioma) and 0.70-0.73 on TCGA-KIRC
(~420 patients, renal cell carcinoma). The standard baselines are
Pathomic Fusion (Chen et al., IEEE TMI 2022) for patient-level 32-d
modality vectors and MCAT (Chen et al., ICCV 2021) for patch-level WSI
features. Both are exactly replicated in this work to provide locked
comparison points.

Recent literature (InterSHAP audit, arXiv 2603.29977, 2026) measures the
cross-modal interaction variance on glioma survival at 3-5% across
architectures spanning c-Index 0.64 to 0.82. This suggests an upper bound
on what any fusion mechanism can extract. The project's experimental
ladder is designed to test that bound systematically.


SECTION 1 — BASELINES (locked and replicated)
---------------------------------------------

Pathomic Fusion (Chen, IEEE TMI 2022). 3 modalities: 32-d VGG histology,
32-d cell-graph CNN, 32-d genomic SNN. Fusion via bilinear gating +
Kronecker tensor product. Cox partial likelihood. Replicated on TCGA-GBMLGG
(0.8174 +/- 0.0717, 15-fold CV; paper reports 0.826) and TCGA-KIRC
(0.7184 +/- 0.0513, 15-fold CV; paper reports 0.720).

MCAT (Chen, ICCV 2021). Per-patch WSI features (up to 30K patches per
slide) co-attended with 6 gene-signature pathway queries. Genomics
serves as Q, WSI features as K/V. NLL-Survival on 4 discrete time bins.
Replicated on TCGA-BLCA (0.6320 +/- 0.0417, 5-fold CV; paper reports
0.624) and TCGA-GBMLGG (0.8197 +/- 0.0201, 5-fold CV; paper reports
0.835).


SECTION 2 — FUSION INTERVENTIONS TESTED (13 trained, 1 designed-only)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

The following thirteen interventions were trained and statistically
evaluated. Each is briefly characterised by its mechanistic family. All
were paired-seed evaluated against the appropriate locked baseline.

Feature-level multiplicative gating family:
  1. GenoFiLM (pre_ln scheme). Genomics-conditioned FiLM modulation of
     the histology + graph features before the bilinear gate. Tested with
     LayerNorm placed before the affine modulation.
  2. GenoFiLM (residual scheme). Same FiLM mechanism with a residual
     connection allowing the model to fall back to the unmodulated
     features.
  3. GenoFiLM (plain scheme). Direct multiplicative + additive FiLM
     without LayerNorm.
  4. FiLM_residual replacement. Replaces the bilinear gate entirely with
     a LayerNorm + FiLM block.
  5. NoGateTrilinear. The Pathomic Fusion trilinear Kronecker product
     with the bilinear gating layer disabled, isolating the contribution
     of the gate.

Low-rank tensor decomposition family:
  6. GBT-Fusion (block-term Tucker decomposition). Replaces the dense
     Kronecker product with a structured low-rank decomposition. DESIGNED
     BUT NOT TRAINED — parked after early saturation evidence accumulated.

Encoder-side family:
  7. UNI2-h encoder upgrade. Replaces the original 32-d VGG histology
     features with 1536-d features from MahmoodLab's UNI2-h vision-Hub
     foundation model, with a LayerNorm + Linear adapter.

Decision-level disagreement family:
  8. DAF (Disagreement-Aware Fusion). Adds signed inter-modality risk
     residuals as auxiliary features to the final hazard prediction.
     Tested with full ablation of the signed-disagreement contribution.

Attention-bias family (MCAT-extension):
  9.  HACA aux=0 (default). Per-patch hazard MLP r_j whose log is added
      as a bias inside the MCAT co-attention softmax. No auxiliary loss.
  10. HACA aux=0.1 attention-pooled. Adds an auxiliary NLL-Survival loss
      on a co-attention-pooled patch representation.
  11. HACA aux=0.1 direct-r. Corrected auxiliary head that supervises
      r_j directly via summary statistics, bypassing the softmax. Diagnosis
      experiment to test whether the previous two were uninformative nulls.

Decision-level evidential fusion family:
  12. GRFN-Pathomic. Replaces Pathomic Fusion's 3.57M-parameter bilinear
      + Kronecker block with a 588-parameter Gaussian Random Fuzzy Number
      combination rule. Each modality emits (mu, sigma^2, h); a context-
      aware reliability head emits per-patient r. Closed-form combination
      yields (mu_f, sigma_f^2, h_f). Cox-PH reformulation of EsurvFusion's
      regression-style architecture.

Loss-function family (CURRENTLY RUNNING):
  13. DSM-Pathomic. Replaces both the Pathomic Fusion fusion block AND
      the Cox loss with a modality-specific Deep Survival Machines
      mixture (Nagpal et al., 2021). 3 modalities × K=4 Weibull components,
      with each component's shape/scale predicted from a single modality.
      ~1956 trainable parameters. 15-fold GBMLGG sweep completed
      2026-05-19: c-Index 0.8180 ± 0.0578 (Δ = +0.0006 vs PF, paired-t
      p = 0.89, 8/15 fold wins); native S(t|x) IBS 0.1038 ± 0.0260
      (Δ = +0.0067 vs PF, paired-t p = 0.23, 7/15 fold wins, NOT
      statistically significant). The pre-registered calibration-gain
      hypothesis is NOT supported. This closes the loss-function lever
      of the three-axis saturation map.

Calibration evaluation (Phase 2c):
   A 15-fold paired calibration audit was performed on GRFN-Pathomic vs
   Pathomic Fusion using Integrated Brier Score (IBS) and Integrated
   Binomial Log-Likelihood (IBLL). Post-hoc Platt scaling on the locked
   Pathomic Fusion baseline was added as the audit-paper-recommended
   reference.

SECTION 4 — KEY FINDING: A SATURATION PATTERN, NOT A FAILURE
------------------------------------------------------------

Across the 13 trained interventions, the mean signed Delta in c-Index on
TCGA-GBMLGG is approximately -0.002 (with standard deviation ~0.01). No
intervention achieved a statistically significant gain. This is consistent
with the InterSHAP measurement that cross-modal interaction variance on
glioma survival sits in the 3-5% band — the data simply does not contain
more interaction signal than this for any fusion mechanism to extract.

The GRFN-Pathomic calibration audit (Phase 2c) adds a SECOND axis of
saturation. We pre-registered the prediction that GRFN's mathematically
calibrated combination rule would improve Integrated Brier Score over
Pathomic Fusion even at equal c-Index. The audit falsified this
prediction with high confidence (paired-t p = 0.005). Mechanistic
diagnosis: when GRFN is trained with Cox partial likelihood, the
calibration channels (sigma^2_f, h_f) are gradient-free because Cox only
sees the scalar risk mu_f. The architectural calibration apparatus is
computed but never trained.

This re-frames the project from a single-axis fusion survey into a
three-axis saturation map:

  Axis            | Verdict so far                 | Status
  ----------------+--------------------------------+-------------------
  Architecture    | 9 fusion families closed       | CLOSED (strong)
  Encoder         | UNI2-h fails to lift c-Index   | CLOSED (48 runs)
  Loss function   | DSM mixture likelihood null on | CLOSED (2026-05-19)
                  | both c-Index and IBS even with |
                  | native non-Breslow evaluation  |

All three pre-specified axes are now empirically closed on TCGA-GBMLGG.
The TOMM August 31 submission will use this three-axis framing as the
load-bearing finding. KIRC cross-cohort confirmation (Phase 3) is the
highest-value remaining experiment.

SECTION 6 — HONEST GAPS AND RISKS
---------------------------------

  i.   Cross-cohort coverage is uneven. TCGA-GBMLGG carries the bulk of
       the saturation evidence (9 interventions). TCGA-KIRC has been
       used only for the Pathomic Fusion baseline replication, and
       TCGA-BLCA only for the MCAT-family HACA experiments. To match the
       precedent set by the closest comparable audit paper
       (11 architectures x 5 cohorts), I need GRFN + DSM tested on at
       least KIRC before submission. This is the highest-priority gap.

  ii.  Statistical power on individual paired comparisons is moderate.
       15-fold CV with a single seed per fold gives n=15 paired
       observations. Some prior interventions (e.g., DAF, FiLM_residual)
       have effect sizes within the noise floor at this sample size.
       A 3-seed extension (45 paired observations) would tighten
       confidence intervals.

  iii. The GRFN calibration falsification, while clean, depends on the
       Cox + sigmoid output activation. The companion experiment (GRFN
       with native log-time Gaussian likelihood) is required to
       confidently attribute the failure to Cox rather than to any other
       architectural decision.

  iv.  The novelty profile of DSM-Pathomic is intentionally modest. DSM
       itself is established (Nagpal et al., 2021). The novelty in this
       work is (a) the modality-specific component parameterisation
       (incremental over MMDCSM, ICCVW 2025), (b) the first application
       of DSM to TCGA-GBMLGG/KIRC with Pathomic Fusion's frozen
       encoders, and (c) the role in the three-axis saturation map. The
       paper's contribution is the experimental design and falsification
       methodology, not any single architecture.
