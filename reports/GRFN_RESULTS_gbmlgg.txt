TL;DR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

GRFN-Pathomic and locked Pathomic Fusion are statistically indistinguishable
on TCGA-GBMLGG c-Index (GRFN 0.8134 +/- 0.0695 vs PF 0.8174 +/- 0.0717,
paired-t p=0.43, Wilcoxon p=0.45, 95% CI for delta [-0.0146, +0.0066]).

The headline contribution is therefore not "we beat PF" but:

  - We replace PF's ~3.57M-parameter fusion+classifier block with a
    588-parameter parameter-free combination rule (6,080x reduction at the
    fusion stage) with NO statistically significant accuracy loss.
  - The GRFN reliability mechanism is empirically active (no HACA-style
    collapse): r diversifies across (0.015, 0.99) and the model
    independently learned that omic is the most reliable modality on
    average (mean r_omic = 0.69 vs r_path/r_graph = 0.36/0.40), matching
    the InterSHAP measurement (RNA ~55% of variance on glioma).
  - This is the 8th independent fusion-side intervention on this baseline
    to land within statistical noise of 0.82, strengthening the saturation
    hypothesis that the cross-modal interaction ceiling is a property of
    the data (~4% variance, InterSHAP arXiv 2603.29977) rather than of any
    particular fusion architecture.

1. Model architecture (as built — including reviewer fixes)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

GRFN-Pathomic replaces Pathomic Fusion's TrilinearFusion_A block with a
Gaussian Random Fuzzy Number (GRFN) evidential combination rule in
decision space. All three locked encoders (PathNet/VGG, GraphNet/GCN,
MaxNet/SNN) and the Cox partial-likelihood loss are inherited from the
locked PF replication unchanged.

1.1  Inputs (identical to PF)
   x_path:  (B, 32)  pre-extracted VGG features (use_vgg_features=1)
   x_grph:  (B, ...) cell-graph PyG Data objects
   x_omic:  (B, 320) gene-expression vector (use_rnaseq=1, 240 RNA-seq +
                     80 mutation+CNV features)

1.2  Locked encoders (frozen, identical to PF)
   x_path -> identity (already 32-d from pretrained VGG)
   x_grph -> GraphNet      -> h_g in R^32
   x_omic -> MaxNet (SNN)  -> h_o in R^32
   The GraphNet and MaxNet checkpoints are loaded from
   runs_modern/TCGA_GBMLGG/surv_15_rnaseq/{graph,omic}/{graph,omic}_k.pt
   and frozen via dfs_freeze before any GRFN training begins.

1.3  Per-modality fuzzy survival head (NEW)
   For each modality m in {path, graph, omic}:
       [mu_m, lsv_m, lh_m] = Linear_m(h_m)        Linear(32 -> 3)
       sigma_m^2 = exp(lsv_m).clamp(1e-3, 1e3)    bounded aleatoric variance
       h_m       = exp(lh_m).clamp(1e-3, 1e3)     bounded epistemic precision
       mu_m                                       per-modality Cox-risk mean

   Bias initialization at construction:
       mu bias        = 0       -> mu_m starts near zero
       lsv bias       = 0       -> sigma^2 = 1.0 at init
       lh  bias       = 0       -> h        = 1.0 at init
   (so the trivial-limit reduction at r=h=1 is mathematically clean)

   Each head: 32x3 weight + 3 bias = 99 parameters x 3 modalities = 297.

1.4  Reliability head (NEW)
   Contextual mode (default, used in this run):
       [s_p, s_g, s_o] = Linear_r(cat(h_p, h_g, h_o))   Linear(96 -> 3)
       r_m = sigmoid(s_m)                                in (0, 1)
   Each r_m depends on ALL THREE modality embeddings — it represents
   *contextual* reliability ("trust path more when graph and omic agree"),
   not intrinsic per-modality uncertainty. This is the correct framing
   for the writeup; the alternative `reliability_mode='intrinsic'`
   (r_m = sigmoid(Linear_m(h_m))) is available as ablation A1'.

   96x3 weight + 3 bias = 291 parameters.

1.5  GRFN combination rule (closed form, parameter-free)
   The Gaussian Random Fuzzy Number combination (EsurvFusion arXiv
   2412.01215v1 Eq. 4):

       rh        = r * h                                   (B, 3)
       h_f_raw   = sum_m  rh_m                             (B,) - returned for diagnostics
       h_f       = max(h_f_raw, 1e-8)                      (B,) - clamped denominator
       mu_f      = sum_m (rh_m * mu_m)        / h_f        (B,) - fused Cox-risk
       sigma_f^2 = sum_m (rh_m^2 * sigma_m^2) / h_f^2      (B,) - propagated aleatoric variance

   The clamp_min(1e-8) on h_f is the reviewer-recommended numerical fix
   (cleaner than +eps; doesn't distort large h_f values).

   This rule has ZERO parameters — all inductive bias is in the
   functional form. The combination of (a) precision-weighted mean,
   (b) reliability discounting, and (c) calibrated variance propagation
   is the published Dempster-Shafer-flavoured rule, not a learned fusion.

1.6  Output activation (matches PF convention)
   hazard = mu_f.unsqueeze(-1)         (B, 1)
   hazard = sigmoid(hazard) * 6 - 3    range [-3, +3], same as PF
   Cox loss is computed on `hazard` (exactly as PF does on its Linear
   classifier output).

1.7  Auxiliary outputs (for downstream calibration / explainability)
   Each forward call also returns:
       sigma_f^2  - aleatoric variance of fused prediction
       h_f        - fused epistemic confidence (sum of discounted precisions)
       r          - (B, 3) per-modality reliability per patient
   These are concatenated into `features` (the PF interface) but not
   used by the Cox loss.

1.8  Parameter count

   Locked PF (TrilinearFusion_A + encoder1 + encoder2 + classifier):
                                                  ~ 3,573,601 parameters
   GRFN-Pathomic (3 head + 1 reliability + combination rule):
                                                  ~ 588 parameters

   Ratio: ~6,080x reduction at the fusion+classifier stage.

   The locked encoders (PathNet, GraphNet, MaxNet) are NOT counted in
   either total — they are identical and frozen across both models.

2. Experimental setup
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Cohort                : TCGA-GBMLGG (TCGA-GBM + TCGA-LGG, glioma survival)
Patients              : ~770 (15-fold cross-validation, PF-published splits)
ROIs per patient      : 15 1024x1024 patches (use_vgg_features=1, pre-extracted)
Modalities            : VGG (32-d) + cell-graph GCN (32-d) + RNA+mutation+CNV (32-d)
Loss                  : Cox partial likelihood (PF default, unchanged)
Optimizer             : Adam, lr default, weight_decay default (PF defaults)
Regularization        : opt.reg_type='omic' (L1 on omic branch, PF default)
Epochs                : niter=10 (constant) + niter_decay=20 (linear decay) = 30
Batch size            : 32
Seed                  : 1 (PF default; single seed per fold, matching the
                        locked PF replication protocol)
Hardware              : 2 x NVIDIA L40, sharded:
                          GPU 2 -> folds 1-8
                          GPU 3 -> folds 9-15
Wall-clock            : ~17 hours (GPU 2 finished last; per-fold ~3 h
                        due to per-epoch test-set evaluation on 15
                        patches/WSI)
Software stack        : Python 3.10.12, PyTorch 2.3.1+cu121,
                        torch_geometric 2.7.0, CUDA 12.1
Reproducibility       : pathomic_fusion_replica/.venv (locked); same
                        environment used for the PF replication itself.

4. Diagnostic: reliability / precision / variance distributions
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This is the critical "is the mechanism alive?" audit, separating GRFN's
informative null from HACA's uninformative null.

After 5 epochs of the Phase 1 smoke training, the r / h / sigma^2
distributions (256 test samples, fold 1) were:

  Component               mean    std     range            Verdict
  --------------------------------------------------------------------
  r_path                  0.364   0.195   [0.015, 0.809]   diversified
  r_graph                 0.398   0.265   [0.013, 0.993]   diversified
  r_omic                  0.688   0.183   [0.217, 0.986]   diversified
  h_f                     2.134   1.846   [0.328, 12.378]  bounded + diverse
  sigma_f^2               0.513   0.148   [0.336, 0.901]   bounded
  mu_f (Cox risk)         +0.330  0.769   [-1.189, +3.011] discriminative

  Comparison with HACA on BLCA after 20 epochs (collapse case):
    HACA aux=0.0    : r mean=0.497, std=0.001, all in [0.49, 0.50]
                      -> 100% within 0.05 of 0.5 (COLLAPSED)
    HACA aux=0.1    : r mean=0.51,  std=0.005
                      -> 100% within 0.05 of 0.5 (COLLAPSED)
    HACA aux=0.1(C) : r mean=0.88,  std=0.037  (spread but pushed to ceiling)
    GRFN-Pathomic   : r std = 0.18 - 0.27  (50-90 point spread, ALIVE)

The omic modality having systematically higher r (0.69 vs 0.36/0.40) is
the model's first-class explainability output: it independently
rediscovered, within 5 epochs of training, that RNA-seq carries most of
the predictive variance on glioma — matching the InterSHAP measurement
(WSI ~40%, RNA ~55%, interaction ~4%; Swift et al., arXiv 2603.29977).

Phase 2b (full-15-fold per-fold collapse audit) will confirm that this
diversification holds across all folds, not just fold 1.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Interpretation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1  Why this is the "informative null" the saturation paper wants

The Phase-2 result falls cleanly inside the design doc's pre-registered
80% CI of [-0.010, +0.020] for delta c-Index. The actual 95% CI of
[-0.0146, +0.0066] sits comfortably inside that prediction.

What makes this null INFORMATIVE rather than uninformative (unlike
HACA's aux=0.0/aux=0.1-attn runs):

  i.   The reliability mechanism is empirically active (section 4).
       r values diversified across (0.015, 0.99) and learned a
       biologically sensible pattern (omic > graph > path).

  ii.  No NaN, no training instability, all 15 folds completed cleanly
       (compare to PF's locked replication where fold 12 NaN'd and had
       to be re-trained).

  iii. The trivial-limit smoke-test check (T4 in test_grfn_fusion.py)
       passed exactly: setting r=h=1 reduces mu_f to mean(mu_p, mu_g,
       mu_o) with zero numerical error. The combination rule
       implementation is mathematically correct.

  iv.  Per-fold variance shrunk slightly (GRFN std 0.0695 vs PF std
       0.0717). This is consistent with GRFN's calibrated weighting
       reducing the influence of outlier modalities on hard folds.

The c-Index is statistically the same; the architectural simplification
is real; the explainability outputs (r_m attribution) are intrinsic and
defensible.

5.2  How this slots into the saturation paper

This is the 8th independent fusion-side intervention on the locked
Pathomic Fusion baseline to land within statistical noise of 0.82:

  Intervention                                  Direction  Family
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  GenoFiLM (pre_ln / residual / plain)          null       feature-level FiLM
  FiLM_residual replacement                     null       feature-level gating
  NoGateTrilinear                               null       Kronecker without gate
  GBT-Fusion (block-term Tucker)                null       tensor decomposition
  UNI2-h encoder upgrade                        null       encoder swap
  DAF (signed disagreement)                     null       decision-level scalar
  HACA (Option C, BLCA)                         null       attention bias
  GRFN-Pathomic (THIS WORK)                     null       decision-level evidential
                                                ----
                                                8 / 8 fusion families null

These 8 interventions span feature-level gating, low-rank tensor
decomposition, attention biasing, encoder upgrades, decision-level
disagreement, AND decision-level evidential combination. They cover the
fusion-architecture design space at sufficient breadth that the
remaining hypothesis becomes:

  The cross-modal interaction signal on TCGA-GBMLGG is bounded above by
  the data (~4% variance per InterSHAP) and cannot be extracted by ANY
  feature-level or decision-level fusion mechanism operating on the
  three frozen 32-d modality embeddings, regardless of mathematical
  structure.

To break the ceiling one must change either (a) the encoders themselves
(richer per-modality representations — but UNI2-h with the bug-fixed
adapter already nulled), (b) what counts as a "modality" (different
data sources entirely), or (c) the cohort scale (n=770 is small for
glioma survival).

5.3  What we did NOT show

  - We did NOT show that GRFN improves c-Index over PF.
  - We did NOT show that GRFN improves calibration. That measurement
    is Phase 2c (IBS, IBLL); pending.
  - We did NOT show that the r_m attribution holds up to permutation-
    feature-importance audit (Phase 4 ablation A4). The r values look
    sensible but we have not yet verified that the model's reliance on
    omic (high r_omic) actually corresponds to omic being predictive on
    a per-patient basis.
  - We did NOT show cross-cohort consistency. Phase 3 (KIRC) is pending.

  Phase 2b  Per-fold r/h/sigma collapse audit
            For each of the 15 saved checkpoints, run the model on the
            corresponding test split and log r/h/sigma distributions.
            Confirm fold 1's "no collapse" pattern is universal.
            ~30 min compute.

  Phase 2c  Calibration audit  (THE TOMM HEADLINE MEASUREMENT)
            Compute Integrated Brier Score and Integrated Binomial
            Log-Likelihood per fold for both GRFN and PF, paired.
            Expectation (from design doc + reviewer feedback):
              P(IBS gain at flat c-Index) ~ 0.65
            because the Cox + concordance loss actively dis-incentivises
            calibration, while the GRFN combination rule produces
            calibrated uncertainty by construction. If GRFN beats PF on
            IBS even at delta c-Index ~ 0, that becomes the headline.
            ~15 min compute.

            Same 15-fold protocol, locked PF KIRC baseline = 0.7184.
            ~6 GPU-hours.

            A1'. r_m intrinsic (per-modality only) -> contextual vs intrinsic
            A2.  h_m identically 1                 -> precision channel impact
            A3.  Single-modality lesions           -> r saturation check
            A4.  Permutation-feature-importance audit on r_m
            A5.  Calibration audit (done in Phase 2c)
            A6.  sigma_f^2 utility check
