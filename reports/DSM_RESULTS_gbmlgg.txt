STATUS

Phase 2 (15-fold paired-seed evaluation of DSM-Pathomic vs locked Pathomic
Fusion baseline on TCGA-GBMLGG) is COMPLETE on both c-Index and the native
S(t|x) Integrated Brier Score. This is the LOSS-FUNCTION lever experiment
of the three-axis saturation map.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
TL;DR  (one paragraph)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

DSM-Pathomic — a 1,956-parameter modality-specific Deep Survival Machines
mixture head trained with parametric Weibull negative log-likelihood,
replacing both Pathomic Fusion's 3.57M-parameter fusion block AND its Cox
partial-likelihood loss — achieves c-Index 0.8180 ± 0.0578 on 15-fold CV.
This is statistically indistinguishable from locked Pathomic Fusion's
0.8174 ± 0.0717 (paired-t p = 0.89, Wilcoxon p = 0.89, 8 / 15 fold wins,
95% CI for Delta = [-0.0093, +0.0106]). On the native S(t|x) Integrated
Brier Score (the calibration-headline measurement, computed directly from
the Weibull mixture WITHOUT Breslow estimation), DSM-Pathomic scores
0.1038 ± 0.0260 vs PF's Cox+Breslow 0.0971 ± 0.0103 — also statistically
indistinguishable (paired-t p = 0.23, 7 / 15 fold wins). The pre-registered
calibration-gain prediction is NOT supported. Combined with the prior nine
architectural nulls and the UNI2-h encoder null, this completes a three-
axis saturation map (architecture / encoder / loss) in which all three
levers are empirically closed on TCGA-GBMLGG.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. The pre-registered hypotheses
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The DSM-Pathomic experiment was specified after the GRFN-Pathomic
calibration audit (Phase 2c, 2026-05-18) cleanly falsified the prediction
that an evidential combination rule would deliver calibration gains under
Cox partial likelihood. The mechanistic diagnosis there was that GRFN's
calibration channels (sigma_f^2, h_f) are gradient-free under Cox: the
loss sees only mu_f, and the Breslow estimator used for IBS only consumes
mu_f as well, so the architectural calibration apparatus is mathematically
discarded.

DSM-Pathomic was specified to test whether the calibration ceiling is
Cox-specific or deeper. Two pre-registered priors:

  (1) P(c-Index gain >= +0.012 vs PF on GBMLGG)          ~= 0.08
      Rationale: even the parametric mixture likelihood is unlikely to
      extract more cross-modal interaction signal than the InterSHAP-
      measured 4% variance ceiling.

  (2) P(IBS gain >= +0.005 vs PF on GBMLGG)              ~= 0.45
      Rationale: DSM produces calibrated S(t|x) by construction — the
      Weibull mixture log-likelihood is the standard cost function for
      calibration-aware survival modeling. If the Phase 2c diagnosis is
      right (Cox is the calibration bottleneck), DSM's likelihood-based
      loss should substantively improve IBS.

  (3) P(at least one of c-Index, IBS gains)              ~= 0.50

The c-Index parity prediction is confirmed (mean Delta = +0.0006).
The IBS-improvement prediction is NOT confirmed (mean Delta = +0.0067
in the wrong direction, but with 95% CI crossing zero; p = 0.23).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Experimental setup
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Cohort                : TCGA-GBMLGG, 770 patients, 15-fold CV (PF-published splits)
Encoders (frozen)     : PathNet/VGG (32-d), GraphNet/GCN (32-d), MaxNet/SNN
                        (32-d). Identical to the locked PF baseline.
DSM head              : 3 per-modality (alpha, beta) heads × K=4 Weibull
                        components + 1 mixture-gate head over the
                        concatenated trimodal embedding.
Trainable parameters  : 1,956  (vs PF's ~3.57M at fusion+classifier stage)
Loss                  : DSM negative log-likelihood
                          L = -mean_i [ event_i * log f(t_i | x_i)
                                        + (1 - event_i) * log S(t_i | x_i) ]
                        where f, S are the Weibull-mixture density and
                        survival functions, computed in log-domain with
                        logsumexp for numerical stability.
Cox-comparable risk   : risk_i = -E[T | x_i] = -sum_{m,k} w_{m,k}(x) * beta_{m,k}
                                  * Gamma(1 + 1/alpha_{m,k})
                        Used for c-Index ranking (concordance is invariant
                        to monotonic transforms; -E[T] preserves ordering
                        of hazards correctly).
Alpha (shape) range   : [0.3, 10.0]  — covers decreasing, constant, and
                        increasing hazard families without numerical
                        pathology.
Beta (scale) range    : [1.0, 10000.0] days — sane for TCGA survival.
Training              : 30 epochs (10 constant + 20 linear decay),
                        Adam optimizer, batch size 32, all other
                        hyperparameters identical to locked PF baseline.
Software stack        : PyTorch 2.3.1+cu121 (NOT auton-survival's 2021-era
                        pinning, which broke the environment on first try
                        — see DSM_Pathomic_ARCHITECTURE.txt section 0.5).
Hardware              : 2 x NVIDIA L40 (46 GB), sharded:
                          GPU 2 -> folds 1..8
                          GPU 3 -> folds 9..15
Wall-clock            : ~15 hours total (per-fold ~1 hour due to the
                        15-patch-per-WSI test-set evaluation; the
                        smoke-train extrapolation underestimated this).

4. Calibration result — native S(t|x) IBS (no Breslow)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Critical methodological note: the IBS values below are computed using
DSM's NATIVE survival function (the Weibull mixture closed-form), NOT a
Breslow estimator on DSM's Cox-comparable risk. Breslow estimation is
inappropriate for DSM — its risks (-E[T] in days) are not "linear
predictors" in Cox's exp(linear_predictor) semantics, and Breslow's
non-parametric baseline estimation overflows / produces degenerate
survival curves when applied. We verified this empirically: the Breslow-
on-DSM-risks attempt failed on 7 of 15 folds with `Input estimate
contains infinity` errors. The native-S(t|x) audit below is the
methodologically correct comparison.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. Interpretation
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6.1  The DSM result is "informative null #3"

Unlike the GRFN calibration audit (which was a clean falsification with
a known mechanism — Cox is calibration-blind), the DSM result is an
informative null: the mechanism IS exercised (Weibull-mixture likelihood,
native S(t|x), no Breslow shortcut), and the calibration still does not
substantively improve. This rules out the "Cox is the bottleneck"
hypothesis. The calibration ceiling is not specific to one loss; it
appears in every tested form.

DSM does deliver a quiet structural benefit (tighter c-Index variance
across folds, +0.0477 on the difficult fold 13), but the mean signal is
not different from PF on either metric.

6.2  Per-fold variability is interesting

Looking at the 15-fold IBS breakdown:
  - DSM beats PF in 7 / 15 folds, including some large wins (fold 4 by
    -0.026, fold 1 by -0.008, fold 5 by -0.011).
  - DSM gets crushed in 2 folds, particularly fold 15 by +0.063.
  - DSM's per-fold IBS std is 2.6x larger than PF's (0.026 vs 0.010).

This means DSM is HIGHER-VARIANCE, not WORSE on average. The mixture
form fits some folds (likely the heavier-tailed LGG mixes) much better
than Cox + Breslow, but a few folds (likely GBM-heavy with short-tail
distributions) are fit worse. This is a publishable side-finding: DSM
is a high-variance bet, not a uniformly-worse competitor.

6.3  What DSM-Pathomic specifically contributes to the paper

  - First Pathomic-Fusion-based DSM result on TCGA-GBMLGG (no published
    precedent; MMDCSM ICCVW 2025 applied DSM to Alzheimer's, not glioma).
  - First demonstration that the calibration ceiling persists under a
    fully parametric calibration-aware likelihood.
  - 1,956-parameter loss-function-side architecture that statistically
    ties a 3.57M-parameter Cox-based fusion. Parameter reduction of
    ~1,827x with no accuracy loss.
  - Per-modality mixture weights w_{m,k}(x) provide intrinsic
    attribution analogous to GRFN's reliability r, but at the
    survival-distribution level rather than the risk-scalar level.
